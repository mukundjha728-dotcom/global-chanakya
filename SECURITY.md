## Reporting a Vulnerability

At Global Chanakya, we take security seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly.

### How to Report

Please include:

- Description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Potential impact
- Screenshots or proof of concept (if available)

### Contact Information

## Responsible Disclosure Guidelines

Please:

- Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability before it has been fixed.
- Do not access, modify, or destroy data belonging to others.
- Do not perform actions that could disrupt our services.
- Provide sufficient information to reproduce the issue.

- All subdomains owned by Global Chanakya
- Public APIs and services operated by Global Chanakya
